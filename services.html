The Nitty Gritty

Everything you need to know about what we offer.

Home Cleaning

Forget the weekend scrub. Our local cleaning pros treat your home like their own. Whether you need a deep seasonal reset or a weekly tidy-up, we bring the shine back to your space using products that are safe for kids and pets.

  • Standard & Deep Cleaning options
  • Eco-friendly supplies included
  • Same-day availability in some areas
📚

Math Tutoring

Algebra shouldn't keep you up at night. Our tutors are local university students and retired teachers who break down complex problems into stuff that actually makes sense. We focus on confidence, not just grades.

  • Algebra, Calculus, and SAT Prep
  • One-on-one or small groups
  • Snacks usually included (if at the Hub!)
🐕

Pet Sitting

Vacations are better when you aren't worried about your furry best friend. We don't do cages; we do cuddles. Your pet stays in a loving home environment with verified animal lovers right in the neighborhood.

  • Daily photo/video updates
  • Administering meds if needed
  • Certified tummy scratchers
